Recently Answered

A selection of real questions from UK nuclear supply chain companies, answered by NucCol specialists.

We're a precision machining company with no nuclear experience — where do we even start?
Start with the Fit For Nuclear (F4N) self-assessment. It scores your business across eight pillars — strategy, quality, people, and so on — against what nuclear buyers expect, and gives you a clear, evidenced plan to close any gaps before you approach a Tier 1 or prime contractor.
Example Getting Started
Do we need ISO 9001 before we can be considered for nuclear work?
Not strictly — but you'll need an equivalent, evidenced quality management system either way. ISO 9001 is the fastest, most recognised route, and most F4N-granted companies hold it, but what matters most to assessors is that your processes are documented, consistently applied, and can be demonstrated on the day.
Example Quality & Standards
How long does the whole F4N process typically take from start to membership?
It varies with your starting point, but most companies take somewhere between six and eighteen months from first self-assessment to a verified, granted membership — the biggest factor is usually how much process documentation and evidence you already have in place before you start.
