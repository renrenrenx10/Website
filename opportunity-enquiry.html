There’s a better route for you than this one

This research works by matching physical products against where components appear in reactor designs. For a service, labour or workforce business there is nothing to place, so the component analysis would come back empty — and the rest would be programme background you can read for free.

The opportunity is still real. It is a different route, and none of it needs Fit For Nuclear:

  • The Sizewell C and Hinkley Point C workforce supply chains procure managed and agency labour continuously, not only at contract award, through EDF’s supplier route.
  • STEP Fusion at West Burton is a decade-long construction and commissioning programme, approached through UKAEA and its delivery partners.
  • Your existing Tier 1 clients are themselves moving workforce between sectors. That is usually the fastest path, because it uses relationships you already hold.

03

CredentialsWe set what you hold against how common it is across the 2,211 companies on our register. This is usually the most revealing part of the research.

Management system certifications you hold
Is design inside the scope of your ISO 9001 certificate?Asked separately because it is queried at pre-qualification more often than any other scope question, and a certificate with design excluded closes positions that otherwise look open.
Product and code certifications
What material certification can you provide as standard?A decisive gate for nuclear material supply, and one of the cheapest gaps to close.
Do you hold a documented counterfeit and fraudulent items procedure?Tier 1 buyers name counterfeit control as a systemic gap in their supply base. Having one documented is a differentiator, not a box-tick.
Approvals and registrations you hold
Trade bodies, networks and research partnershipsThese shape how we recommend you approach a target. A shared membership turns a cold registration into a warm introduction, and that is often the difference between an action that gets done and one that gets filed.
Can you handle security-cleared personnel or export-controlled material?
We haven’t asked for certificate numbers, issuing bodies or exact scope wording. They matter, but chasing them turns a ten-minute form into an hour at the filing cabinet. We’ll come back to you for the two or three that actually change the answer.
