About this self-assessment

Thinking about whether nuclear work is realistic for your business, or just want a clear view of how ready you are for regulated, high-integrity work in general? This self-check is a quick, honest way to find out. It is based on the seven categories F4N (Fit For Nuclear) uses to assess UK manufacturers: Nuclear Industry Fundamentals, Nuclear Safety Culture, Quality & Compliance, Leadership & Governance, Human Performance, Supply Chain Readiness, and Security of Information.

Each question below has a short note explaining why it matters. Read it even if you are unsure how to answer, because understanding the "why" is often more useful than the tick itself. There is no pass mark. It exists purely to help you have an honest conversation with yourself and your team about where you actually stand today.

Private to you. Your answers stay in your own browser and are not sent to NucCol. Use Reset at the top to clear them.

What to do with your result
  • A lower score is not bad news. It simply tells you where a proper assessment is likely to add the most value first.
  • Share this with a colleague or two and compare answers. Differences in how people at different levels of the business see things are often the most useful finding of all.
  • Use the category names above to see where you are strongest and weakest. If quality, traceability or supply chain came up short, the Traceability & Documentation and Spotting Counterfeit Parts guides are good next reads.
  • Revisit this in three to six months. Readiness is something you build over time, and tracking the change is more useful than a single snapshot.
